StockMaster Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

StockMaster Pro ("the app") is a business management app for tracking stock, sales, suppliers and staff. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is protected.

1. Data stored on your device

The business data you enter (products, sales, suppliers, staff and related records) is stored on your own device. The developer does not collect it and does not have access to it.

2. Google Drive backup

If you choose to back up your data, the app asks for permission to use Google Drive. The app uses only the drive.appdata permission, which gives it access to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.

  • Backups are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded.
  • Backups are saved only in your own Google Drive, in a private folder that only StockMaster Pro can access.
  • The developer cannot read, access or recover your backups.
  • The app cannot see your other Google Drive files.
  • The permission is used only to create, update, download and delete the app's own backup file.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

StockMaster Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing of data

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party. Data from Google Drive is not used for advertising.

5. Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • You can remove the backup from your Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps (in Google Drive), or revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

7. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, email tenkolsstockmaster@gmail.com.
